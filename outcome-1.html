ENC 1102 · LEARNING OUTCOME 01

Generating Inquiry

Developing questions about writing, communication, and how people adapt their language to different audiences.

ARTIFACT 01

Research Proposal

This research proposal helped me explore how audience, purpose, and expectations influence the way college students write. My interest came from my own experiences with assignment-based writing, where I sometimes feel that formal writing expectations make it harder to communicate my thoughts clearly. Reading Sadie Raghunand’s “The Rhetoric of BookTok” also got me interested in how different communities develop their own ways of communicating. These experiences led me to question whether students change their writing because they want to communicate effectively or because they want to meet expectations and earn a grade.

Reflection: My Research Process

Developing this proposal pushed me to look beyond my personal experiences and turn my observations into a research question I could investigate. I had to consider how I could collect useful information by asking students about their audiences, motivations, tone, and effort. One question I became especially interested in was whether students put more effort into writing when they have a personal reason to do it rather than when it is simply required for an assignment. This artifact demonstrates my genuine inquiry because it began with something I personally noticed and developed into a question I wanted to investigate through research and student responses.

ARTIFACT 02

Google Survey

My Google Survey helped me explore how college students change their writing depending on their audience and purpose. I created hypothetical situations involving professors, friends, bosses, classmates, and family members to see how students might respond differently in each situation. I also included questions about tone, effort, and motivation because I wanted to understand not just how students change their writing, but why they make those choices.

Reflection: Turning Questions into Research

Creating the survey pushed me to turn my general curiosity into questions that other students could actually answer. One challenge was choosing situations and answer choices that could reveal meaningful differences in how people write. I also wanted to look beyond my own experiences and see whether other students noticed similar patterns. This artifact shows my genuine inquiry because I used my original question to guide a practical way of collecting information. It also helped me recognize that writing choices can be influenced by several factors, including the audience, the purpose, and whether someone is writing out of personal interest or obligation.
